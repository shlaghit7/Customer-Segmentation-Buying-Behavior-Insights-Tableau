# Customer Segmentation & Buying Behavior Insights in Tableau

## Overview

Customer segmentation and buying behavior analysis are critical for businesses seeking to improve customer retention, personalize marketing campaigns, and maximize revenue. This Tableau project provides an interactive dashboard that analyzes customer demographics, purchasing patterns, loyalty behavior, and spending trends to support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard helps identify high-value customers, understand product preferences, evaluate regional spending behavior, and uncover actionable insights for marketing and business teams.

---

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to:

* Segment customers based on spending and purchasing behavior.
* Analyze buying patterns across different customer groups.
* Identify high-value and repeat customers.
* Evaluate product category performance.
* Understand regional spending trends.
* Support marketing and customer retention strategies through data visualization.

---

## Business Problem

Businesses often face challenges in understanding:

* Who their most valuable customers are.
* Which customer segments contribute the highest revenue.
* Which products are preferred by different customer groups.
* How customer behavior varies across regions.
* Which customers are likely to become inactive.

This dashboard addresses these challenges through customer analytics and interactive visualizations.

## Key Calculated Fields

### Total Revenue

SUM([Total Spending])

### Average Order Value

AVG([Average Order Value])

### Total Customers

COUNTD([Customer ID])

### Repeat Customer %

Percentage of customers with more than one purchase.

### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Average Order Value × Purchase Frequency × 12

### Loyalty Score

Purchase Frequency + Customer Satisfaction Score

## Key Business Insights

### Customer Segmentation

* High-Value Customers contribute the majority of total revenue.
* Loyal Customers show higher retention rates.

### Product Performance

* Electronics and Fashion categories generate the highest sales.

### Regional Analysis

* Northern region demonstrates the highest spending levels.

### Customer Behavior

* Repeat customers contribute significantly to overall revenue.
* Customers with higher income levels tend to spend more.

### Strategic Recommendations

* Focus retention programs on High-Value Customers.
* Offer loyalty rewards to repeat customers.
* Personalize marketing campaigns based on customer segments.
* Increase promotions in underperforming regions.
